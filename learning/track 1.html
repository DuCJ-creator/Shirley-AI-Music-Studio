📐 5 Simple Sentence Structures | 五大句型結構分析

🟦 1. S + V

結構：主詞 + 不及物動詞

  • Dust danced.
  • The creek flows.

🟢 2. S + V + O

結構：主詞 + 及物動詞 + 受詞

  • The sun warmed our backs.
  • We built our world.

🟡 3. S + V + SC

結構：主詞 + 連綴動詞 + 主詞補語

  • We were young and wild.
  • Those pebbles felt like gold.

🟣 4. S + V + IO + DO

結構：主詞 + 授予動詞 + 間接受詞 + 直接受詞

  • That water gave me dreams.
  • You handed me a twig.

🔴 5. S + V + O + OC

結構：主詞 + 動詞 + 受詞 + 受詞補語

  • That summer made us brothers.

💡 Rhetorical Devices | 修辭手法分析

1. Simile | 明喻

使用 like 或 as 直接比較兩個事物。

  • Where the dragonflies shone like jewels.
  • Those pebbles felt like gold.
  • The air smelled like rain.

2. Metaphor | 隱喻

隱含地將一事物說成是另一事物，進行間接比較。

  • A sword against the mundane. — 將「回憶/勇氣」隱喻為一把劍。
  • But we, a marching band! — 將「夥伴」隱喻為樂隊。

3. Personification | 擬人化

賦予非人類事物人類的特質或行為。

  • Dust danced on that road.
  • The old tree shared its secrets.
  • Time forgot all the rules.
  • That country creek still whispers...

4. Apposition | 同位語

將一個名詞性短語置於另一名詞旁邊，對其解釋說明。

  • You handed me a twig, a token arcane.
