📐 Simple Tenses | 簡單時態：過去、現在、未來

🟦 1. 現在簡單式 (Present Simple)

結構： S + V(原形/三單) + …

  • I walk this road alone.
  • Time watches silently.
  • The sun rises in the east.

🟢 2. 過去簡單式 (Past Simple)

結構： S + V(過去式) + …

  • I stumbled yesterday.
  • I lost my words to say.
  • The rain fell hard and cold.

🟡 3. 現在簡單式 (be動詞)

結構： S + be(am/is/are) + …

  • I am who I need to be.
  • I was brave, or so I'm told.
  • The past is a lesson learned.

🟣 4. 未來簡單式 (Future Simple)

結構： S + will + V(原形) + …

  • I will rise again.
  • I will face the wind.
  • My dreams will light the way.

🔴 5. 時態對比與應用

概念：透過歌詞理解時間軸

  • 過去：I stumbled / I lost / The rain fell
  • 現在：I walk / I am / Time watches
  • 未來：I will rise / I will face / I will be stronger
  • 時間副詞：yesterday, tomorrow, today, now

💡 Rhetorical Devices | 修辭手法分析

1. Personification | 擬人化

賦予非人事物以人類的動作、情感或特質。

  • Time watches silently.
  • The clock's hands never wait.
  • The clock's hands never lie.
  • Time will have its say.

2. Metaphor | 隱喻

隱含地將一事物比作另一事物，不使用 like 或 as。

  • The past is a lesson learned.
  • The present is a fire burned.
  • The future is a bridge to cross.
  • My dreams will light the way.

3. Repetition | 重複

重複關鍵詞或句子，以加強語氣與節奏感。

  • TIME TELLS! — 重複出現的副歌主題
  • I chose my path! I chase my dream! I'll find my way! — 平行結構的重複
  • With every gain and every loss! — 對比性重複

4. Parallelism | 平行結構

使用相同或相似的語法結構，增強節奏與對比。

  • I chose my path! I chase my dream! I'll find my way!
  • The past is... The present is... The future is...
  • I'll face the wind, I'll fight the rain.
