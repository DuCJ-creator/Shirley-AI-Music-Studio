📐 Tense Contrast | 時態對比：現在、過去、未來

🟦 1. 現在進行式 (Present Progressive)

結構： S + am/is/are + V-ing + …

  • The city lights are blinking.
  • Raindrops are falling softly.
  • I am becoming someone.
  • I am living this story!

🟢 2. 過去進行式 (Past Progressive)

結構： S + was/were + V-ing + …

  • The winds were howling loudly.
  • I was searching for an answer.
  • You were waiting at the station.
  • Trains were coming, going.

🟡 3. 未來進行式 (Future Progressive)

結構： S + will be + V-ing + …

  • I will be walking different streets.
  • I will be speaking a new language.
  • The sun will be rising on the chapters.
  • I will be becoming more.

🟣 4. 混合時態對比

結構：歌詞中的時態對比

  • 現在進行：I'm living / I'm finding / You're witnessing
  • 過去進行：I was dreaming / The past was shaping
  • 未來進行：I will be soaring / The future will be calling
  • 時間對比：yesterday / right now / tomorrow

🔴 5. 歌曲核心時態結構

概念：透過歌詞理解時態轉換

  • 過去：I was dreaming yesterday / The past was shaping me
  • 現在：I'm living this story / The present is making me
  • 未來：I will be soaring tomorrow / The future will be calling me
  • 核心句：Right now, I'm finding my way!

💡 Rhetorical Devices | 修辭手法分析

1. Personification | 擬人化

賦予非人事物以人類的動作、情感或特質。

  • Time is whispering secrets.
  • The future will be calling me.
  • The winds were howling loudly.
  • The world will be watching.

2. Metaphor | 隱喻

隱含地將一事物比作另一事物，不使用 like 或 as。

  • The past was shaping me. (往昔塑我形)
  • The present is making me. (當下鑄我魂)
  • My dreams will light the way.
  • Raindrops tracing lines upon the glass.

3. Repetition | 重複

重複關鍵詞或句子，以加強語氣與節奏感。

  • Right now… — 重複出現的主題
  • I'm living this story! — 副歌開頭重複
  • Can you feel the world… — 預副歌重複結構
  • Every second, life is learning — 節奏性重複

4. Parallelism | 平行結構

使用相同或相似的語法結構，增強節奏與對比。

  • I was dreaming yesterday! I'm living right now! I will be soaring tomorrow!
  • The past was shaping me! The present is making me! The future will be calling me!
  • In this breath, in this hour.
  • New sights and new sounds.
