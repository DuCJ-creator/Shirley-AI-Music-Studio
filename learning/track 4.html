📐 Perfect Tenses | 完成時態：現在、過去、未來

🟫 1. 現在完成式 (Present Perfect)

結構： S + have/has + V(過去分詞) + …

  • I have walked these winding roads.
  • I have learned to sing a hopeful tune.
  • Time has painted wisdom on my cheek.
  • Every choice has built the person I'm today.

🟤 2. 過去完成式 (Past Perfect)

結構： S + had + V(過去分詞) + …

  • I had fallen long before I learned to fly.
  • I had sworn I couldn't trust anymore.
  • You had carried scars you never let me see.
  • We had built our separate walls.

🟡 3. 未來完成式 (Future Perfect)

結構： S + will have + V(過去分詞) + …

  • I will have built a home beneath the stars.
  • I will have turned the final page.
  • I will have learned...
  • BY THE TIME I SAY GOODBYE!

🔵 4. 完成進行式 (Perfect Continuous)

結構： S + have/has been + V-ing + …

  • I have been changing!
  • I had been breaking!
  • I will have been becoming!
  • I have been collecting pieces of the light.

🟤 5. 完成時態對比與應用

概念：透過歌詞理解時間關係

  • 現在完成：have walked / have learned / has painted
  • 過去完成：had fallen / had sworn / had carried
  • 未來完成：will have built / will have turned
  • 時間標記：by the time / long before / when

💡 Rhetorical Devices | 修辭手法分析

1. Metaphor | 隱喻

隱含地將一事物比作另一事物，不使用 like 或 as。

  • Life is a mystery. (人間至美 早藏玄機)
  • Every loss has gently worn the sharp away. (得失磨我 棱角生光)
  • Time has painted wisdom on my weary cheek. (時光落筆)
  • We had built our separate walls. (各築心垣 各守孤寂)

2. Personification | 擬人化

賦予非人事物以人類的動作、情感或特質。

  • Time has painted wisdom on my cheek.
  • Every loss has gently worn the sharp away.
  • Pages started turning in the tale.
  • Life had kept its beauty as a mystery.

3. Parallelism | 平行結構

使用相同或相似的語法結構，增強節奏與對比。

  • I have lived... I had dreamed... I will have learned...
  • I have been changing! I had been breaking! I will have been becoming!
  • Every choice has built... Every loss has gently worn...
  • Right from the prologue to the closing.

4. Repetition | 重複

重複關鍵詞或句子，以加強語氣與節奏感。

  • I HAVE LOVED WITH ALL MY HEART! — 副歌重複主題
  • Every choice... Every loss... — 強調性重複
  • Pages turn... Moments burn... — 開頭平行重複
  • BY THE TIME I SAY GOODBYE! — 結構性重複
