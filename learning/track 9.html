§ 📐 Grammar | 副詞子句全解析

1. 時間副詞子句 (Time)

表達動作發生的先後順序或同時性。

  • When / While / As: When I was falling... / While I was hiding... (當...之時)
  • Before / After: Before I knew... / After the darkness... (在...之前/後)
  • Since / Until: Since I met you... / Until you found me... (自從.../直到...)
  • Whenever: Whenever I stumble... (每當...時)

2. 讓步副詞子句 (Concession)

表達「雖然、儘管」，用於強調主句結果的出乎意料。

  • Though / Even when: Though I was broken... / Even when I doubted... (雖然/即使)
  • Even if: Even if I fall again... (即便再次...)

3. 原因與條件 (Reason & Condition)

  • Because / Now that: Because you loved me... / Now that I'm stronger... (因為/既然)
  • If / As long as: If I keep moving... / As long as I'm breathing... (如果/只要)

4. 地點副詞子句 (Place)

  • Wherever: Wherever I wandered... / Wherever life takes me... (無論去哪裡)
§ 💡 Rhetoric | 修辭意象

1. Metaphor: The Wall (牆的隱喻)

"I built a wall so tall": 牆象徵自我封閉、防禦心強，不想讓他人看見自己的脆弱。

2. Contrast: Darkness & Morning (明暗對比)

"After the darkness, the morning always came": 用自然的日夜交替比喻人生的低谷與轉機，傳達希望感。

3. Personification: Life (擬人化)

"Wherever life takes me": 將「命運/生活」賦予主動引領的能力，表達對未來的開放心態。
